# Fastfood Flavor – Unity
# Overview

Fastfood Flavor is a charming 2D cooking game.
You play as the chef of a fast-food restaurant, combining available ingredients to prepare delicious dishes for hungry customers!

# Key Features

- Cute and lively 2D graphics

- Friendly and accessible gameplay suitable for all ages

- A rich ingredient system, with new items unlocked as you level up

- Increasing speed and difficulty as more customers arrive

- Performance-based scoring system: fail the level if you don't meet the required score!

- Star rating system: your final score determines your star rating—will you be fast enough to earn all stars?

# How to Play

- Combine available ingredients to prepare fast-food dishes for customers waiting at the counter.

- Some ingredients require cooking or additional preparation.

- Use simple drag-and-drop interactions to assemble different ingredients.
